João Afonso

design, photography

work

is a multidisciplinary artist and designer originally from Madeira Island, currently based in Lisbon. Working at the intersection of photography and design, his practice is guided by careful composition, visual sensitivity, and a minimalist approach. He explores different forms of balance and visual restraint, seeking harmony not only as an aesthetic outcome, but as both method and meaning, allowing simplicity and equilibrium to define his visual language.
